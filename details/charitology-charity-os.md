## Overview

Charitology Charity OS is a free, MIT-licensed open-source operational workspace built for small and mid-sized charities and nonprofits. It organizes day-to-day programs, volunteers, grant periods, public request forms, confirmed participation bookings, consented email notices, and immutable grant-period reports without vendor lock-in. It pairs with Charitology's open Global Charity Index (31 jurisdictions, 3.34M official registry records) and a verified directory of 65 free software donations, grants, and nonprofit perks.

## Target Audience

- Grassroots Nonprofits
- Small and Mid-Sized Charities
- Volunteer-Run Organizations
- Civic and Community Groups

## Features

- Volunteer & Participation Management
- Public Request Forms & Own-Request Portal
- Confirmed Participation Bookings & Manual Waitlists
- Grant-Period Tracking & Immutable Grant Reports
- Full JSON Backup & Restore Export (No Vendor Lock-In)
- Read-Only Public MCP Server (`https://os.charitology.org/mcp`) for AI Assistants
- Self-Hostable MIT Codebase (`https://github.com/theguysaccount/charitology`)

## Pricing Model

Free and open source (MIT license). Available hosted at [os.charitology.org](https://os.charitology.org/) or self-hosted from GitHub at zero software cost.
